Senior Software Engineer, Backend Infrastructure
Google — Bengaluru, Karnataka, India

About the job
As a Senior Software Engineer at Google, you will design, build and operate
large-scale distributed backend systems that serve millions of users. You will
work across the full stack of our services platform and partner with product and
infrastructure teams to ship reliable, high-throughput services.

Minimum qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience.
- 5 years of experience with software development in Java, Go or C++.
- 3 years of experience building distributed systems and microservices.
- Experience with data structures and algorithms, and strong system design skills.
- Experience designing and operating RESTful APIs in production.

Preferred qualifications
- Master's degree in Computer Science or a related technical field.
- Experience with event-driven architectures and stream processing using Apache Kafka.
- Experience with Kubernetes, Docker and CI/CD pipelines.
- Experience with cloud platforms (GCP, AWS) and infrastructure as code (Terraform).
- Experience with observability tooling, monitoring and incident response / on-call.
- Experience mentoring junior engineers and leading technical design reviews.
- Experience with SQL and NoSQL datastores at scale, and performance tuning.

Responsibilities
- Design and implement highly available, low-latency backend microservices.
- Own services end to end, including on-call, monitoring and incident response.
- Drive technical design reviews and mentor junior engineers on the team.
- Collaborate with cross-functional partners to define APIs and service contracts.
- Improve reliability through caching, circuit breaking and capacity planning.
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines and automated testing for your services.
